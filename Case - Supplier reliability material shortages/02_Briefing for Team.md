# Assignment Brief

## Role

You are an analytics task force advising Asterion Motion Systems' Chief Supply Chain Officer. Your task is to diagnose supplier reliability problems and material shortage patterns across critical material groups, then recommend a focused intervention program.

## Team Setup

Each team should assign clear ownership for:

- Data quality and supplier-performance diagnostics.
- Shortage, lead-time, and production-impact analysis.
- Planning-parameter, sourcing, and management-recommendation design.

Teams may use Excel as the primary tool. Python, R, Power BI, or similar tools are permitted for data preparation, visualization, and robustness checks.

## Suggested Milestones

### Kick-off Session

- Understand the business context and constraints.
- Inspect the data structure and assign team roles.
- Form initial hypotheses about supplier reliability and shortage drivers.

### Online Meeting 1

- Present a data-quality assessment.
- Show preliminary supplier-performance indicators by supplier, material group, plant, and criticality.
- Identify records that should be excluded, corrected, or flagged.

### Online Meeting 2

- Present material-shortage and production-disruption patterns.
- Link shortage outcomes to supplier reliability, lead-time variability, planning parameters, quality holds, and transaction anomalies.
- Draft the intervention logic and expected management impact.

### Final Session

- Deliver a management recommendation.
- Defend the analytical method and practical feasibility.

## Core Questions

1. Which suppliers show the most severe reliability problem after adjusting for material criticality and order volume?
2. Which material groups contribute most to production shortage risk?
3. Where do late deliveries, partial receipts, quality holds, and planning parameters appear to reinforce each other?
4. Which plants, production lines, suppliers, or materials should management prioritize?
5. Are observed shortage events primarily structural, process-driven, supplier-driven, or data-quality-driven?
6. What planning-parameter or sourcing-policy changes could reduce shortages without overinvesting in inventory?
7. Which data-quality issues materially affect management conclusions?
8. What KPI dashboard should Asterion introduce for purchasing, production planning, and supplier management?
9. What is the estimated financial opportunity from the recommended intervention?
10. What risks or unintended consequences could arise if management acts on your recommendation?

## Minimum Analytical Expectations

Your analysis should include:

- A reproducible data-cleaning logic.
- A purchase-order and goods-receipt reconciliation by supplier, material, plant, and date.
- Supplier-reliability indicators, such as on-time delivery rate, confirmation reliability, partial-delivery rate, quality-hold rate, and lead-time variability.
- Shortage indicators, such as shortage days, affected production orders, lost output, expedite cost, and downtime cost.
- A comparison across material groups, supplier risk classes, plants, and production lines.
- At least three visualizations suitable for a management audience.
- A prioritized intervention roadmap with expected impact and implementation effort.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your work will be evaluated on:

- Business relevance and clarity of recommendation.
- Quality of data handling and transparency of assumptions.
- Ability to distinguish supplier performance problems from planning and data-quality noise.
- Analytical depth relative to the available data.
- Quality of visual communication.
- Strength of Q&A defense.

## Presentation Guidance

Do not present every table you created. Management needs the answer, the evidence, and the action. Use appendices for technical details, data-cleaning rules, and sensitivity checks.
