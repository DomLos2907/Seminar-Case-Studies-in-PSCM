# Assignment Brief

## Role

You are an analytics task force advising LumaHome Online's Chief Operations Officer. Your task is to diagnose why returns are eroding margin and creating operational pressure, then recommend a focused returns-management intervention program.

## Team Setup

Each team should assign clear ownership for:

- Data quality, product mix, and return-rate diagnostics.
- Inspection capacity, backlog, refund speed, and process-flow analysis.
- Resale recovery, value-loss modelling, and management-recommendation design.

Teams may use Excel as the primary tool. Python, R, Power BI, or similar tools are permitted for data preparation, visualization, and robustness checks.

## Core Questions

1. Which product categories, customer segments, and channels create the highest return burden after adjusting for sales volume and value?
2. Where does limited inspection capacity create the largest backlog and delay?
3. Which delays matter most for resale recovery and value loss?
4. Which return reasons and condition grades should receive faster triage?
5. Are observed losses primarily demand-side, policy-driven, operations-driven, product-quality-driven, or data-quality-driven?
6. Should LumaHome change return-policy rules, customer communication, inspection routing, staffing, or refurbishment strategy?
7. Which products or return flows should be prioritized for fast-lane processing?
8. Which data-quality issues materially affect management conclusions?
9. What is the estimated financial opportunity from the recommended intervention?
10. What risks or unintended consequences could arise if management tightens return policies or changes refund timing?

## Minimum Analytical Expectations

Your analysis should include:

- A reproducible data-cleaning logic.
- An order, return, inspection, refund, and resale reconciliation by product, customer segment, date, and return center.
- Return indicators such as return rate, return reason mix, late-return share, condition-grade mix, and serial-returner exposure.
- Operations indicators such as queue days, inspection cycle time, capacity utilization, backlog growth, refund lead time, and manual-review rate.
- Financial indicators such as refund value, recovery value, value loss, write-off share, refurbishment cost, and margin impact.
- A comparison across product categories, sales channels, promotion types, fulfillment centers, return centers, and customer segments.
- At least three visualizations suitable for a management audience.
- A prioritized intervention roadmap with expected impact and implementation effort.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your work will be evaluated on:

- Business relevance and clarity of recommendation.
- Quality of data handling and transparency of assumptions.
- Ability to distinguish return-rate problems from inspection and remarketing bottlenecks.
- Analytical depth relative to the available data.
- Quality of visual communication.
- Strength of Q&A defense.

## Presentation Guidance

Do not present every table you created. Management needs the answer, the evidence, and the action. Use appendices for technical details, data-cleaning rules, and sensitivity checks.
