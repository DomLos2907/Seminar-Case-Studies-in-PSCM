# Assignment Brief

## Role

You are an analytics task force advising Veridian Home & Living's Chief Supply Chain Officer. Your task is to diagnose whether the company's transport tendering strategy is creating hidden cost, service, and sustainability problems, then recommend a revised carrier allocation and governance approach.

## Team Setup

Each team should assign clear ownership for:

- Data quality, lane structure, and tender-award diagnostics.
- Shipment execution, tender acceptance, spot-market, and service-performance analysis.
- Cost, CO2, and management-recommendation design.

Teams may use Excel as the primary tool. Python, R, Power BI, or similar tools are permitted for data preparation, visualization, and robustness checks.

## Core Questions

1. Which carriers appear cheap in the tender but expensive after service failures, spot substitution, claims, and CO2 deviations are considered?
2. Which lanes or lane groups show the most severe cost-service-sustainability trade-off?
3. Where do tender rejections, missed appointments, and spot-market purchases reinforce each other?
4. Which awarded carrier shares should management reconsider?
5. Is the observed performance problem primarily carrier-driven, lane-driven, tender-governance-driven, demand-driven, or data-quality-driven?
6. How should Veridian define a balanced carrier score that does not overreward the lowest rate?
7. What changes to allocation rules, mini-tender triggers, penalty clauses, or mode choices could improve outcomes?
8. Which data-quality issues materially affect management conclusions?
9. What is the estimated financial and CO2 opportunity from the recommended intervention?
10. What risks or unintended consequences could arise if management reduces volume from low-cost carriers?

## Minimum Analytical Expectations

Your analysis should include:

- A reproducible data-cleaning logic.
- A shipment, tender-award, and spot-quote reconciliation by lane, carrier, date, and customer segment.
- Carrier indicators such as tender acceptance rate, on-time delivery rate, average delay, missed-appointment rate, claim rate, spot substitution rate, and CO2 target deviation.
- Cost indicators such as contracted cost, actual paid cost, spot premium, accessorial cost, claim cost, and estimated cost of service failures.
- A comparison across lane groups, carrier types, tender ranks, customer segments, and transport modes.
- At least three visualizations suitable for a management audience.
- A prioritized intervention roadmap with expected impact and implementation effort.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your work will be evaluated on:

- Business relevance and clarity of recommendation.
- Quality of data handling and transparency of assumptions.
- Ability to distinguish tender price from true transport cost.
- Analytical depth relative to the available data.
- Quality of visual communication.
- Strength of Q&A defense.

## Presentation Guidance

Do not present every table you created. Management needs the answer, the evidence, and the action. Use appendices for technical details, data-cleaning rules, and sensitivity checks.
